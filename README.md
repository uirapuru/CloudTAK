> **taklab fork.** This is a modified version of CloudTAK v13.101.1 used by taklab (branch `taklab/v13.101.1`), published under the AGPL-3.0 as required. Changes: a paperclip button in one-to-one chats attaches a map snapshot (`y-taklab-map` CoT event) for the ai-buddy chat bot, and 3D buildings from Cesium ion as map overlays (routes `/api/ion`, `/api/ion/:name/endpoint` and `/api/ion/geoid`, the last returning the EGM96 geoid undulation used to lower tilesets onto the terrain, admin config key `ion::token` with env var fallback `CESIUM_ION_TOKEN`, overlay type `3dtiles` rendered via deck.gl; a terrain overlay (admin `map::terrain`) must be configured for correct footprint placement). The production CSP in `api/nginx.conf.js` allows the Cesium ion and Google tile hosts and `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` for the meshopt decoder. ZXY basemaps and overlays also accept WMS GetMap URLs with the MapLibre `{bbox-epsg-3857}` variable, filled with the Web Mercator extent of each proxied tile, and `{bbox-epsg-4326-latlon}` (minLat,minLon,maxLat,maxLon, the WMS 1.3.0 axis order) for services without EPSG:3857. Live layers: routes `/api/live` and `/api/live/:name` proxy the taklab live-feeds service named by `LIVE_FEEDS_URL`, the Overlay Explorer lists them under "Na żywo", and overlays in mode `live` refresh their GeoJSON every `frequency` seconds (capped at 600 s); feature properties starting with `_` style the feature and are hidden from the properties table. Live layers v2: `/api/live/:name?since=` answers `{"unchanged": true}` when nothing changed, and `/api/live/:name/:id` returns feature details (properties, a photo, a route); live overlays draw point icons (bus, tram, plane, tower, shelter), lines and polygons from the `_icon`, `_color`, `_label`, `_rotation`, `_width` and `_fill_opacity` properties, and their styles always come from code, never from the saved profile; the feature view shows the photo and a "Pokaż trasę"/"Ukryj trasę" toggle. ZXY tile proxying waits 15 s and retries once. The production CSP `img-src` also allows `https://t.plnspttrs.net` for aircraft photos from Planespotters. Live feature details may carry `route_label` (e.g. "zasięg"), shown on the toggle button, and polygon routes are drawn as a semi-transparent fill; the CSP `img-src` also allows `https://maps.googleapis.com` for Street View thumbnails. Overlay groups: table `profile_overlay_groups` and routes `/api/profile/overlay/group` store named, per-user groups server side (overlay field `group_id`); in the Overlays menu "Dodaj grupę" adds a group, the grip on the left of each row drags overlays between groups and within a list, the grip in a group header reorders the groups (group `pos`), and a group checkbox shows or hides all its overlays. The top of the Overlays menu is the top of the map stack: Map Features, the groups (a new group is listed first), the ungrouped overlays, the basemap. A new overlay is listed at the top of the ungrouped overlays (drawn above them, below the groups) and the positions are saved, so the order survives a reload; "Dodaj widoczne do nakładek" keeps the listed order. Overlays are deleted without a confirmation step, also from "Usuń widoczne z nakładek". Overlay categories and favorites: the Overlay Explorer lists every tile overlay (all pages and collections), live layer and 3D building in collapsible sections by category, in a fixed order (Ulubione, Teren, Woda, Pogoda i powietrze, Lotnictwo, Łączność, Infrastruktura, Transport, Ratownictwo i bezpieczeństwo, Turystyka, Zdjęcia lotnicze, Budynki 3D, Inne; unknown categories follow alphabetically). The category of a tile overlay is its basemap `collection`, of a live layer the optional `category` field of the `/api/live` index (now passed through by the proxy), 3D buildings are "Budynki 3D", anything else "Inne". The search filters inside the sections; the collapse state is kept per device. "Dodaj/Usuń widoczne" work on the open sections, and on every match while searching. A star next to each layer, in the Explorer and the Overlays menu, marks it as a favorite: table `profile_overlay_favorites` (migration 0220; the fork migrations sit between upstream ones: 0216 groups, 0217 incoming email, 0218-0219 upstream core entity, 0220 favorites, ordered by their journal timestamps) and routes `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/profile/overlay/favorite` store `<mode>:<mode_id>` keys per user, with a local copy for offline use. Favorites are listed again in "Ulubione" at the top of the Explorer and, as shortcuts to the same overlays (visibility and star only, outside drag and map order), in a collapsible section at the top of the Overlays menu. An overlay added from the Explorer goes on top of the group named after its category, created when missing and placed among the other category groups in category order; "Ułóż w kategorie" in the Overlays menu moves every ungrouped overlay to its category group (overlays the catalog no longer lists stay ungrouped). Catalog overlays already in a profile take the current catalog name when the Explorer or the Overlays menu loads.
